    原题¶

    Complete the following sentences with the words in the box. Change the form if necessary.

    词汇释义¶

    Word POS English definition 中文释义
    pertaining to prep. relating to; concerning 关于；与……有关
    synchronous adj. occurring at the same time; happening together 同步的
    rationale n. the reasons or logical basis for a decision or belief 理由；依据
    fabrication n. the invention of false information or evidence 捏造；伪造
    holistic adj. considering a whole system rather than separate parts 整体的；全面的
    resonate with v. to evoke a shared response or agreement in someone 与……产生共鸣
    idiosyncratic adj. peculiar to an individual; distinctive and unusual 独特的；个人特有的
    differentiate v. to recognize or show the difference between things 区分；区别
    perpetuate v. to cause something to continue indefinitely 使延续；使持续
    ethics n. moral principles governing conduct 伦理；道德准则
    manuscript n. an author's text before publication; a written document 手稿；文稿
    pronounced adj. very noticeable or strongly marked 明显的；显著的
    aggregate v. to combine or collect into a whole 汇总；合计
    vagueness n. lack of clear detail or precision 模糊；含糊
    leverage v. to use something to maximum advantage 利用；借助

    参考答案¶
    1. rationale 9. fabrication
    2. pertaining to 10. holistic
    3. vagueness 11. manuscript
    4. leveraging 12. aggregated
    5. synchronous 13. perpetuates
    6. pronounced 14. resonate with
    7. differentiate 15. ethics
    8. idiosyncratic

    拼写提醒

    • pertaining to 是介词短语，句中不随主语变形。
    • leverage → leveraging（第 4 题）、aggregate → aggregated（第 12 题）、perpetuate → perpetuates（第 13 题）都要按句法改变形式。
    • vagueness 与 fabrication 都是不可数名词，前面通常不加 a/an。
